Off = not running · Paper = recorded and measured, no alerts · Live = alerts on. A live strategy is auto-muted if its last 100 settled signals are clearly below break-even. Judge a strategy only after a few hundred settled signals.

How this strategy works

Five factors vote on the last closed candle: Bollinger band bounce (30), RSI divergence (25), Stochastic %K/%D cross (20), CCI extreme + reversal (15), candlestick pattern (10). A signal is issued only when at least 3 factors agree and opposing weight is small; otherwise SKIP. The signal is for the next candle and is settled automatically when it closes (win if price closed in the signalled direction). "Strength" is a heuristic 0–100 — it is not a win probability. The win accuracy above is what actually happened, with a 95% interval, compared with the break-even rate implied by the payout.
