An honest track record

Most signal services show a win rate and stop. Tradzen shows what the number is worth.

  • Win rate with its range. A small sample gets a wide range, not a big claim.
  • Break-even built in. Every record is compared with the win rate your payout needs.
  • Muted automatically. A strategy that is clearly losing stops alerting until it recovers.
  • Paper first. New ideas prove themselves on paper before they are allowed to alert anyone.

Questions

Short, straight answers.

Do the signals make money?

No one can promise that, and we do not. Many strategies do not beat break-even, and the dashboard shows it. Use the records to decide what to follow, and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.

Does Tradzen place trades for me?

No. It is read-only: it observes your own Quotex tab to show alerts and keep your journal. You place every trade yourself.

Is it safe to install?

The extension only runs on Quotex pages, sends prices (or just your own trades) to your Tradzen account over HTTPS, and never reads your Quotex login. You can revoke its access token any time on your account page.

How do I get access?

Accounts are created by the team. {{HOW_TO_ACCESS}}

How do I install the Chrome extension?

Download it with the button at the top of this page, unzip the file, open chrome://extensions in Chrome, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Then click the Tradzen icon, open Connection, sign in with your account and reload your Quotex tab. It only reads your own Quotex tab and never trades or clicks.

Which browser do I need?

Google Chrome (or a Chromium browser) on a computer, with your Quotex tab open while you trade.

Is it available in my country?

Binary and digital options services are restricted or banned in some countries. Check the rules that apply to you before you use any trading tool.
